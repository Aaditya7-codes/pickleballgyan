Accountability

Corrections

Material factual errors are corrected promptly and transparently. If a correction changes the meaning of a page, the page will carry a dated correction note describing what changed. Minor spelling, grammar or formatting fixes that do not change meaning may be made without a note.

Submit a correction or venue update

Email pickleball.hero7@gmail.com with the page URL or venue name, the specific correction, and a public supporting source where possible. You do not need a GitHub account. Do not send sensitive personal information.

Submissions are reviewed; they do not automatically change a listing or article. We may seek clarification or retain the current wording where the available evidence does not establish a correction.

Accountability

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected promptly and transparently. When a correction changes the meaning of an article, the article should carry a dated correction note describing what changed.

Minor spelling, grammar or formatting fixes that do not change meaning do not require a correction note.

Send a correction

A public corrections email address will be added before the publication begins regular reporting.

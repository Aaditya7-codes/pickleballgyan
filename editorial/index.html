Standards

Editorial standards

Pickleball Gyan aims to create a reliable public record of Indian pickleball. Accuracy, source quality and independence take priority over publishing volume.

Sourcing

Primary sources are preferred: rulebooks, tournament regulations and results, manufacturer specifications, governing-body documents, venue records, government or corporate filings, and direct interviews or verification. Press releases and social posts are treated as claims, not automatic facts.

Data

Participation, court and market figures should state date, source, scope and methodology. Records are labelled verified, reported or estimated where appropriate. Missing information is not invented.

Gear

A product is never described as tested unless it has been physically tested using a documented method. Before that point, coverage relies on specifications, approval records, verified Indian pricing, warranty/support terms and clearly sourced independent measurements.

Commercial relationships

Sponsored content, advertising, affiliate relationships and paid venue enhancements must be clearly identified. Editorial rankings and conclusions are not for sale.

# Editorial publication checklist

Use this before publishing or materially updating any Pickleball Gyan page.

## Evidence

- [ ] The page has a clear reader need and adds India-specific value.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has a dated, attributable source or is visibly qualified.
- [ ] Primary sources were preferred; organisational claims are attributed as such.
- [ ] Numbers state their date, scope, definition and method where relevant.
- [ ] Unknown facts are omitted rather than inferred.

## Independence and clarity

- [ ] Editorial analysis, reported facts and opinion are distinguishable.
- [ ] Commercial relationships are disclosed and do not affect conclusions.
- [ ] Gear coverage does not imply testing without a documented method.
- [ ] Venue pages do not imply a visit, endorsement or live availability.

## Maintenance

- [ ] Title, summary, canonical URL and internal links are accurate.
- [ ] Fast-changing fields show a check date.
- [ ] Sources and evidence labels are visible where needed.
- [ ] Material errors can receive a dated correction note.
- [ ] The page has an update trigger or review date.

Editorial standards →
Primary sources first

Rulebooks, official results, manufacturer records, filings and direct verification before secondary retelling.

India-specific

No automatic import of US prices, availability, pathways or market assumptions.

No invented data

Unknown means unknown. Estimates are labelled, dated and accompanied by methodology.

Commercially independent

Advertising, affiliate relationships and enhanced listings never buy editorial conclusions.

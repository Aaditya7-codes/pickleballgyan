Methodology

Every structured record should carry enough metadata for another reader to understand where it came from and how fresh it is.

Core record fields: source, date checked, geographic scope, verification status, material definitions and notes on uncertainty.

Verification labels

Verified means checked directly against a primary source or directly confirmed. Reported means attributed to an identifiable external source but not independently confirmed. Estimated means calculated from disclosed assumptions or incomplete evidence.

Updates

Fast-changing information such as prices, venue status, tournament schedules and product availability should show a date checked. Older records remain useful only when their historical date is visible.

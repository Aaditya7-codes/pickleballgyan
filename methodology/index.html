Research

Methodology

Every structured record should show where it came from, how fresh it is and what we have not established. A listing is never a recommendation, site visit, endorsement or guarantee of availability.

Core record fields: source, source type, date checked, geographic scope, verification status, material definitions and notes on uncertainty.

Evidence labels

Verified
Checked against a current public primary venue/operator source or directly confirmed.
Platform-reported
Attributed to a current identifiable booking platform, not independently confirmed.
Estimated
Calculated from disclosed assumptions or incomplete evidence.
Unverified
A research lead; not published as a directory record.

Source hierarchy

  1. Current official venue/operator pages, booking pages and official business channels.
  2. Current third-party platform listings, visibly labelled.
  3. Organiser pages or reputable reporting, attributed and used only where relevant.
  4. Reader submissions as leads, never as published facts without an attributable source.

Updates and corrections

Fast-changing information such as prices, venue status, schedules and product availability shows a check date. To report an update, email pickleball.hero7@gmail.com with the relevant URL, correction and supporting public source. Read the full corrections policy.

About

India’s independent guide to pickleball.

Pickleball Gyan is an independent publication focused on explaining how pickleball is played, organized, bought and built in India.

It is not a governing-body website, booking platform, registration service or promotional arm of a league, venue or equipment brand. The publication is designed for players, parents, coaches, organizers, buyers, operators, brands, investors and journalists who need information they can interrogate and verify.

What we cover

Play, gear, the Indian pickleball scene, original data and verified places to play. We prioritise work that creates durable public records rather than high-volume rewrites of announcements.

What independence means

Commercial relationships do not buy favourable editorial conclusions. Claims from organizations and manufacturers are identified as claims unless independently verified. Material errors are corrected transparently.

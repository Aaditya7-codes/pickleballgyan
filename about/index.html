About

India’s independent guide to pickleball.

Pickleball Gyan is an independent publication explaining how pickleball is played, organised, bought and built in India. It is not a governing-body website, booking platform, registration service or promotional arm of a league, venue or equipment brand.

Founder and editor

Pickleball Gyan is founded and edited by Aaditya Sharma. Editorial decisions, rankings and inclusion decisions are independent of advertising, affiliate relationships and paid commercial products.

What we cover

Play, gear, the Indian pickleball scene, original data and source-labelled places to play. We prioritise work that creates a durable public record over high-volume rewrites of announcements.
